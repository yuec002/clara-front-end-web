Public Notice No. 17

The Object Has Arrived

It will remain here until further notice.

At 6:42 this morning, an object was discovered in the center of the courtyard. No one has explained where it came from.

It measures approximately three feet across, makes no detectable sound, and appears different depending on where you stand.

What We Know

    It was not there yesterday.
    It has no visible opening.
    It is warm to the touch.
    Birds will not approach it.
“Please resist the temptation to decide what it is before you have spent some time looking at it.”

Visitors

The courtyard will remain open from sunrise until sunset. You may observe the object for as long as you wish.
